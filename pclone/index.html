          Built for here

          Global suites were built for somewhere else. You've been paying for the gap.

          The big platforms are excellent at what they were designed for. Then you spend a year and a local partner bolting on statutory payroll, chasing currency differences, and training staff to approve things on a laptop they don't carry. pclOne starts from the conditions you actually work in.

          • The naira moved again this week.

            Books, budgets and project margins carry the transaction currency and your reporting currency side by side, so a client paying in dollars and a supplier billing in cedis land in the same P&L without a side spreadsheet.

          • Payroll has a dozen statutory lines.

            Pension, housing funds, levies and PAYE are set up per entity and per country. When rules change, you update the rule once, not every payslip.

          • Approvers are rarely at a desk.

            Leave, purchase orders and invoices are approved from a phone in two taps, with the full context attached. The audit trail records who approved what, and when.

          • Your people grow through appraisals, not job titles.

            pclHRM was shaped by a firm that runs performance management for some of the region's largest employers. Appraisals, development plans and skills feed straight into capacity planning.

          • Regulators ask for evidence, not intentions.

            pclComply reviews policy documents against your frameworks in about a minute and keeps every change on record, so audit week stops being a scramble.

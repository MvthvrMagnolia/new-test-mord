          Built for the conditions you actually work in.

          The global platforms are excellent at what they were designed for. Then you spend a year and a local partner bolting on statutory payroll, chasing currency differences, and training staff to approve things on a laptop they don't carry. You've been paying for that gap.

          • The naira moved again this week.

            Books, budgets and project margins carry the transaction currency and your reporting currency side by side. A client paying in dollars and a supplier billing in cedis land in the same P&L without a side spreadsheet.

          • Payroll has a dozen statutory lines.

            Pension, housing funds, levies and PAYE are set up per entity and per country. When rules change, you update the rule once, not every payslip.

          • Approvers are rarely at a desk.

            Leave, purchase orders and invoices are approved from a phone in two taps, with the full context attached. The audit trail records who approved what, and when.

          • People grow through appraisals.

            pclHRM was shaped by a firm that runs performance management for some of the region's largest employers. Appraisals, development plans and skills feed straight into capacity planning.
